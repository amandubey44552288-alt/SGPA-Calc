How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ (Semester Credits)

For example, if your semesters have different credit totals, each semester is weighted according to the number of credits you completed.

How is SGPA calculated?

SGPA stands for Semester Grade Point Average. It measures your academic performance during a single semester.

SGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits

Each subject contributes to your SGPA according to its credit value. A subject with more credits therefore has a greater effect on your semester SGPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SGPA?

SGPA stands for Semester Grade Point Average. It represents your academic performance for a particular semester.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

How is SGPA calculated?

SGPA is calculated by multiplying each subject's credit by its grade point, adding the results, and dividing by the total number of credits.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA can be calculated by taking the credit-weighted average of your semester SGPAs.
